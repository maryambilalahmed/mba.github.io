This essay examined the relationship between women’s employment conditions and fertility outcomes in garment-sector contexts. The central challenge was to connect labour-market structure with household decision pathways.

I focused on separating correlation from mechanism in the argument: workplace formality, social constraints, and economic bargaining power can each alter fertility decisions through different channels.

The project strengthened my academic writing discipline and source evaluation process.

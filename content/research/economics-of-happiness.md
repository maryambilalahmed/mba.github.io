This research asks how people in different socioeconomic contexts distinguish happiness from satisfaction. We are building a mixed-methods framework that combines interview data with economic theory.

The fieldwork has highlighted recurrent themes: security, dignity, social trust, and agency. While these are often discussed qualitatively, our objective is to systematize them without losing nuance.

The project is ongoing and forms a central part of my academic trajectory.

This internship gave me early exposure to how robotics strategy is built from first principles. I focused on repetitive workflows where automation could improve reliability, reduce fatigue-related error, and support scalable operations.

My process combined use-case mapping, competitor scanning, and feasibility comparisons. Instead of treating "robotics" as a broad category, I framed each application around context, task frequency, and value capture.

The most useful lesson was methodological: strategy quality depends on how clearly we define the problem before proposing a solution.

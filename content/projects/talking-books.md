Talking Books was designed to make learning personal and memorable. Rather than presenting ideas through one-directional lectures, we invited speakers to share lived stories and encouraged thoughtful questions from participants.

The event focused on creating a safe and respectful environment where curiosity could lead to perspective change. For many participants, this format made abstract ideas more relatable and easier to remember.

The project strengthened my belief that communication design is a core part of educational impact.

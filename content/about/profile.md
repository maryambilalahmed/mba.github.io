I am an A-Level student at Lahore Grammar School with a long-term interest in economics, social systems, and public-facing research.

My current work focuses on happiness economics in Pakistan, where I am part of a mixed-methods effort combining interviews with conceptual analysis. I care about field-based evidence and careful interpretation, especially when a topic is often discussed in abstract terms.

Alongside research, I build projects that involve leadership, communication, and execution. I have worked on social-impact initiatives, student entrepreneurship activities, and early robotics strategy exposure through internship work.

I am building this portfolio as a living academic record: a place where projects, writing, and research are documented with clarity, context, and accountability.
